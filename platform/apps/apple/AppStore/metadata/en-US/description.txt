The Cardsite app puts your addresses in your pocket and on your desktop.

SHORT URLS PEOPLE REMEMBER
Give any web address a short one on card.site — card.site/Spring-Menu, not a string of characters. Keep the random address the app suggests or choose your own.

QR CODES THAT CAN CHANGE AFTER PRINTING
Point a QR code at one of your short links, then change the link's destination whenever you like. Every printed copy follows it. Share or save the image straight from the app.

SEE WHAT HAPPENS AFTER THE CLICK
Daily views, visitors, top referrers and countries for every link, over 7, 30 or 90 days.

EVERY URL HAS A CARD
Add @ to any Cardsite address to see it as a summary card. Open any link's card from the app.

YOUR PAGES, READY TO SHARE
Bio pages you build on cardsite.com are listed in the app, one tap from the share sheet.

BUILT FOR EACH DEVICE
A tab bar on iPhone, a sidebar on iPad, menus and keyboard shortcuts on Mac. Light and dark appearance, Dynamic Type and VoiceOver throughout.
