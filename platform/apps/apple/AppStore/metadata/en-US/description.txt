Cardsite® is a presence platform. Create, host and share pages, links, QR codes, files and more in seconds, and see what happens after every click.

The Cardsite app brings your whole account to iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Vision Pro and Apple Watch.

SHORT LINKS PEOPLE REMEMBER
Give any web address a short one on card.site: card.site/Spring-Menu, not a string of characters. Keep the random address or choose your own. Then shape it with a schedule, display rules by country, device or language, a password, a click limit, an expiry, UTM tags, cloaking or a splash page.

QR CODES THAT CAN CHANGE AFTER PRINTING
Build a QR code for a web address, Wi-Fi network, contact, event, email, text message, location and more, with your own colours and a live preview. Point one at a short link and change the destination whenever you like: every printed copy follows it.

BIO PAGES YOU EDIT ANYWHERE
Add, arrange and edit the blocks on your bio page, with a live preview beside the editor. Set the theme, branding, SEO and who sees the page.

EVERY KIND OF LINK
Contact cards that save straight to Contacts, events that save to Calendar, payment links, file links, static pages and splash pages, each with its own short address.

SEE WHAT HAPPENS AFTER THE CLICK
Pageviews and visitors for every link and page over 7, 30 or 90 days, with breakdowns by country, city, referrer, device, browser, operating system, UTM campaign and hour.

ORGANISE AND WORK TOGETHER
Group resources into projects, serve links from your own domain, attach pixels, and switch between workspaces. Invite members, set their roles, and follow the activity feed and your inbox.

READY WHEREVER YOU ARE
• Widgets show a link's clicks or your QR code on the Home Screen and the Mac desktop.
• Shorten any page from the share sheet in Safari or any app.
• Ask Siri to shorten a link, show your QR code or count your clicks.
• Show a full-screen QR code from Apple Watch.
• Use menus, keyboard shortcuts and a command palette on iPad and Mac, and a native window on Apple Vision Pro.

SECURE BY DEFAULT
Set up two-factor authentication, change your password, manage API tokens and review sign-in activity from the app.

Light and dark appearance, Dynamic Type and VoiceOver throughout. Sign in with your Cardsite account or create one in the app. You can delete your account from the app at any time.
